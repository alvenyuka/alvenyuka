## How I work

- **Start from the decision.** The right measure depends on what a mistake costs: precision when a false
  fraud flag freezes a customer's money, a readable reason when a declined applicant is owed one.
- **Check the numbers outside the tool that made them.** Models are compared against standard libraries,
  spreadsheets are recomputed in Python, and the checks themselves are tested by breaking copies on purpose.
- **Say what the evidence does not support.** Every project lists its limitations, including the ones that
  change the headline.
