# Alven Yuka

Data Scientist and Financial Analyst, Nairobi. I build and check credit-risk, fraud and valuation models.

I am a CPA Finalist and an Accounting Specialist at GIZ Kenya, where I have worked for three years on a donor-funded portfolio of 12 programmes, with zero audit findings across two review cycles. There I rebuilt a budget-variance report in Power BI so it takes two hours instead of three days. Outside work I build models in Python and Excel on the same principle: every figure has to trace back to the code that produced it, and I say where each result stops being reliable.

## Projects

| Project | What it does | Data | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Credit Risk Scorecard](https://github.com/alvenyuka/Credit-Risk-Scorecard) ([live demo](https://credit-risk-alven.vercel.app)) | A points-based score that tells a lender which applicants are likely to default, with a reason for every decline | Home Credit Default Risk: 307,511 applications | Approving only the top 80% by score cuts credit losses by 47% (AUC 0.754 on 61,503 held-out applicants) |
| [Fraud Detection System](https://github.com/alvenyuka/Fraud-Detection-System) ([live demo](https://fraud-detection-alven.vercel.app)) | Flags fraudulent mobile-money transfers so a fraud team can freeze them | PaySim, simulated: 6.36 million transactions | Stops 99.98% of fraud value on the holdout, with 3 false alarms |
| [Kiva Loans Microfinance Analytics](https://github.com/alvenyuka/Kiva-Loans-Microfinance-Analytics) | Ranks new microloans by the risk they never get fully funded | Kiva: 671,205 loans | The riskiest 10% of loans hold 76% of the unfunded dollars |
| [SACCO Stress Test](https://github.com/alvenyuka/SACCO-Stress-Test) | Estimates a Kenyan SACCO's IFRS 9 expected credit loss under three economic scenarios | Synthetic: 2,000 invented loans, no real SACCO data | Loss rises from KES 1.81M (base) to KES 19.27M (severe); capital ratio falls to 6.7% |
| [Financial-Analyst](https://github.com/alvenyuka/Financial-Analyst) | Excel valuation model of Apple, checked by a separate Python program | Apple 10-K filings, fiscal 2021 to 2025 | Base case $139.50 a share against a $338.40 market price |
| [Stock Portfolio Tracker](https://github.com/alvenyuka/Stock-Portfolio-Tracker-Analytics-Engine) | Excel workbook that tracks a 16-stock portfolio from its trades and measures how concentrated it is | Paper portfolio: 112 trades | NVIDIA is 45.1% of the portfolio; a 30% fall in semiconductors would cost $39,480 |
